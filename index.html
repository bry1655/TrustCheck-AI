      If you think you have been scammed

      1. Stop. Do not send more money or share your PIN, password or verification code.

      2. Verify. Check your actual transaction history using the official app or service.

      3. Preserve evidence. Keep messages, transaction references and receipts.

      4. Report. Contact your mobile money provider, bank or the appropriate official reporting channel.

      5. Secure accounts. Change compromised passwords and contact the affected provider.
