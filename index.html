      If you think you have been scammed

      1. Stop. Do not send more money or share your PIN, password or one-time verification code.

      2. Verify. Check your actual transaction history using the official app or service. Do not trust a screenshot alone.

      3. Preserve evidence. Keep messages, transaction references, phone numbers and receipts.

      4. Report. Contact your mobile money provider, bank or the appropriate official Kenyan reporting channel.

      5. Secure accounts. Change compromised passwords and contact the affected provider immediately.
